# Example: Idea to Campaign — Oat Milk Latte

## Input

**Raw idea:** "We have a new oat milk latte."
**Audience:** Young professionals, 25–35.
**Goal:** Drive 500 orders in 30 days.
**Channel:** Instagram Reels, in-store, email.

## Step 1 — Creative Brief

**Background:** The coffee shop's afternoon sales have dropped 20% over two quarters, and competitors now offer plant-based options.

**Objective:** Generate 500 first-time orders of the oat milk latte in the first 30 days.

**Target audience:** Professionals age 25–35 who buy coffee daily but default to dairy. They want a lighter, modern option that still feels like an indulgent café treat. Barrier: fear that plant milk is thin or chalky. Motivation: feeling smart and current without giving up comfort.

**Key insight:** Customers choose oat milk to feel modern and intentional, not to make a health sacrifice.

**Big idea:** "Your afternoon, lightened."

**Message pillars:** (1) indistinguishable creamy texture; (2) only three ingredients; (3) steady afternoon energy without the crash.

**Tone:** Warm and optimistic, with light wit.

**Mandatories:** shop logo, #CleanAfternoon tag, plant-based certification mark.

**Call to action:** "Order the oat milk latte in the app and get 20% off your first afternoon order."

**Deliverables:** 15-second vertical video, 3 carousel cards, 1 in-store poster, 1 email announcement.

**Metrics:** first-time orders, coupon redemption rate, social engagement rate, repeat afternoon purchase rate.

**Timeline:** script by day 3, shoot by day 7, approval by day 10, launch by day 14.

## Step 2 — Three Creative Directions

### Direction 1: "The Light Switch"
- **Emotional register:** Relief and calm.
- **Core message:** Your afternoon stops being heavy.
- **Execution example:** A 15-second video shows a dim, sluggish 3 p.m. desk scene; the protagonist takes one sip of the oat milk latte and the room brightens, posture lifts, and time speeds into a productive flow.
- **Tone:** Warm and cinematic.
- **Audience fit:** Speaks to the universal afternoon slump and frames the product as the effortless fix.

### Direction 2: "Better by Default"
- **Emotional register:** Confidence and pride.
- **Core message:** The smarter choice is now the easy choice.
- **Execution example:** Three carousel cards compare the heavy afternoon ritual with the oat milk latte: card 1 "No heavy feeling", card 2 "No weird aftertaste", card 3 "Just your afternoon, lightened."
- **Tone:** Playful and direct.
- **Audience fit:** Targets the professional who wants to optimize choices without overthinking.

### Direction 3: "Ritual, Reimagined"
- **Emotional register:** Comfort and nostalgia.
- **Core message:** Keep the ritual, swap the weight.
- **Execution example:** A 15-second spot shows the same daily coffee order through four decades — 1980s, 1990s, 2000s, today — each era's cup gradually changing until it becomes the new oat milk latte. Voiceover: "Every era has its afternoon ritual. This one's light."
- **Tone:** Sophisticated and story-driven.
- **Audience fit:** Appeals to people who love café culture but want a modern update.

## Step 3 — Recommendation

**Best direction:** "The Light Switch." It has the clearest before/after structure, is easy to execute as a short vertical video, and directly addresses the audience's afternoon slump barrier with a visual transformation that needs little copy.

## Step 5 — Assumptions & Open Questions

- **Assumption:** The audience primarily buys through the mobile app; if not, the call to action should change.
- **Assumption:** The shop already has product photography; if not, add a photography dependency to the timeline.
- **Open question:** Does the brand have any existing tagline or voice guidelines that the tone should match?
