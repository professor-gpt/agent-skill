# Executive Terminology & Risk Taxonomy

## Risk Severity Levels
Use these classifications consistently across all deliverables.

| Level | Label | Criteria | Escalation |
|-------|-------|----------|------------|
| **HIGH** | HIGH | Could significantly impact revenue, reputation, compliance, or strategic objectives. Requires immediate executive attention. | Flag for executive decision within 24 h. |
| **MEDIUM** | MEDIUM | Manageable with mitigation; may affect timeline or budget but not overall success. | Recommended to review within the project cadence. |
| **LOW** | LOW | Minor impact; can be handled within existing plans. | Monitor; no escalation needed. |

## Decision Types & Language
- **Strategic Decisions** — long‑term direction, resource allocation, market entry. Use language: “shifts competitive position”, “alters 3‑year roadmap”, “requires board alignment”.
- **Tactical Decisions** — medium‑term implementation choices (vendor selection, budget reallocation). Use: “impacts quarterly targets”, “needs VP sign‑off”, “can be reversed within X months”.
- **Operational Decisions** — day‑to‑day process improvements, scheduling. Use: “improves efficiency by X %”, “requires team lead confirmation”.

## Action & Recommendation Verbs
- **Recommending:** “Adopt”, “Invest in”, “Prioritise”, “Postpone”, “Stop” — always prefaced with “Based on the evidence, we recommend…”. Never “We must”.
- **Flagging:** “⚠️ REQUIRES HUMAN APPROVAL”, “RISK: …”, “ASSUMPTION: …”, “MISSING: …”.
- **Drafting next steps:** “Confirm”, “Assign”, “Draft”, “Schedule”, “Review”.

## Common Corporate Domains (for contextualisation)
- **Finance:** budget, P&L, return on investment (ROI), cost of delay, working capital.
- **Legal:** liability, indemnification, force majeure, IP ownership, regulatory compliance.
- **HR:** FTE, contractor status, performance improvement plan, succession planning.
- **Sales:** pipeline, conversion rate, ARR, churn, account expansion.
- **Product:** roadmap, MVP, technical debt, feature adoption.

## Structuring Evidence & Assumptions
- **Facts** — cite the source: “per the Q3 report (p. 8)”, “in the email from CTO on 12 Jun”.
- **Assumptions** — label with a clear condition: “[ASSUMPTION: based on typical QBR date of last Tuesday of the month]”.
- **Missing Information** — “[MISSING: attendee list — needed to prepare personalised brief]”.
- **Uncertainty** — “[UNCERTAIN: forecasted revenue — ranges $1.2‑1.5M; recommend sensitivity analysis]”.

## Approval Gate Wording
Always include a prominent gate for any recommendation that involves spend, legal commitment, HR action, or external communication.  
Template: `⚠️ **REQUIRES HUMAN APPROVAL** — [who should approve, e.g., “CFO”, “Your final sign‑off”] before proceeding.`

## Confidentiality Headers
All internal-use deliverables must open with: `Status: [INTERNAL CONFIDENTIAL]`  
Never transmit internal‑only analysis to external parties without explicit human permission.
